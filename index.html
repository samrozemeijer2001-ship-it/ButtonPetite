Lafbekken

A party game for one phone. Do you play it safe?

Pass one phone around the table. Every turn you pick LAF and play it safe, or LEF and go all in. The braver the table gets, the wilder it gets.

Caffeine

How much caffeine is still in your tank?

Log a coffee, tea or energy drink in one tap and watch the tank slowly empty during the day. It shows an estimate of how much caffeine is still in your system, and whether the next one will keep you up tonight.

NotNow

Get it out of your head. I'll remember it.

A thought pops up while you're busy? Throw it on a card, type it or just say it, and swipe it away. NotNow keeps it safe until you have time, and nudges you when it matters.

Who's on bathroom duty this week?
