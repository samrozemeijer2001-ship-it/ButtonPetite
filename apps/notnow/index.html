About

A thought pops up while you're busy? Throw it on a card, type it or just say it, and swipe it away. NotNow keeps it safe until you have time, and nudges you when it matters.

Why I made it

My head works like a browser with forty tabs open. While I'm doing one thing, five other things pop up: call the plumber, that book title, buy oat milk. If I don't write them down, they're gone. If I do, I lose my focus. With the hand on the heart, I made NotNow for myself first. Get the thought out of your head in a few seconds, then get back to what you were doing. The app remembers it, so you don't have to.

What you can do

  1. 01

    Capture in seconds

    Type your thought on a card, or press the big yellow button and just say it, in English or Dutch. Swipe right to keep it, left to let it go.

  2. 02

    Mark what matters

    Star a thought and it stays on top of your Thought Bank, so the important stuff never sinks.

  3. 03

    Get a nudge at the right time

    Drag the clock to the hour you want, for today or tomorrow. With lock screen reminders on, the nudge comes even when the app is closed.

  4. 04

    Add a place

    Tag a thought with a place, like home, the office or the laundromat, so you know where it belongs.

  5. 05

    Your Thought Bank

    Everything you kept, in one place. Search it, open a card, mark it done, pin it or let it go.

  6. 06

    A daily recap

    Pick a time for each day of the week. Until then a little sun counts down. Then you see what's still waiting, what's coming up and what you finished today.

  7. 07

    A fresh start every morning

    Thoughts you mark as done stay for the rest of the day and clean themselves up overnight.

  8. 08

    No account, works offline

    Open it once and it keeps working without internet. There's nothing to sign up for. There's a dark mode too.

Questions

How do I put NotNow on my home screen?

iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap the share button and choose Add to Home Screen. Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the menu and choose Install app. Then open NotNow from your home screen.

Why don't I get reminders on my lock screen?

Lock screen reminders need iOS 16.4 or newer, and only work when you open NotNow from your home screen, not from Safari. Then go to Settings and turn on Notifications. On Android it works in Chrome.

Can it remind me when I arrive somewhere?

Not yet. A web app isn't allowed to check where you are in the background, so places are a label for now. I'm looking for a way around it.

Where did my done thoughts go?

Thoughts you mark as done stay for the rest of the day and are cleaned up the next day. That's on purpose: a fresh start every morning.

Voice input doesn't work. What now?

Voice uses the speech recognition of your browser. It works in Safari and Chrome, not in Firefox, and sometimes not in an app on the iPhone home screen. You can always just type.

I added NotNow to my home screen and my thoughts are gone.

Nothing is lost. On iPhone the home screen app has its own storage, separate from Safari. Your old thoughts are still in Safari.

Is it finished?

Not yet, it's a beta. Most things work, some things I'm still building. If something breaks, nothing at the hand: send me a message and I'll fix it.

Need help?

Something broken or confusing? Send me a message. Tell me what happened and which phone you use, and I'll get back to you.

Privacy

Your thoughts stay on your phone. Only if you turn on lock screen reminders, a copy goes to my reminder server, and turning them off deletes it again.
