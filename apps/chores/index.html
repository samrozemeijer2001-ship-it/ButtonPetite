About

A chore tracker for a shared house. Everyone sees whose turn it is, ticks off their task when it's done, and the whole house sees it right away.

Why I made it

In a house with four people, the cleaning schedule on the fridge always lost. Nobody looked at it, and every week we had the same "wasn't it your turn?" discussion. So I made a little app for our house: open it, see your task, tick it off. With the hand on the heart, the strikes are my favourite part. Nobody wants three.

What you can do

  1. 01

    See whose turn it is

    The home screen shows the week, how many days are left and one card per housemate, with their task for this week.

  2. 02

    Know how to do it

    Tap your card and you get your task with short instructions, so "clean the bathroom" means the same thing to everyone.

  3. 03

    Tick it off

    Done? Press the big button. Everyone in the house sees it right away, on their own phone.

  4. 04

    Strikes for the slow ones

    Not done by Sunday night? You get a strike, up to three. Do your task on time and one strike disappears again.

  5. 05

    Faces that judge you a little

    Every card has a face that changes with how the week is going, from happy to full panic when Sunday gets close.

  6. 06

    A party when you're done

    Finish your task and you get a little confetti moment. You earned it.

  7. 07

    House rules in one place

    The house rules are one tap away, so nobody can say they didn't know.

  8. 08

    Everybody does everything

    The tasks rotate every week, so everyone does every job once in a while.

Questions

Can I use it for my own house?

Not yet. Right now it's made for my own house, with our tasks and our schedule built in. If more people want it, I'd love to make a version where you set up your own house. Want that? Send me an idea.

Why can't I open the app?

Because it shows the names of my housemates, it's not public. I only show it here so you know what I'm working on.

Does it send reminders?

Not yet. For now the faces do the nagging.

What language is it in?

Dutch, because that's what we speak at home. An English version would come with the version for everyone.

Need help?

Something broken or confusing? Send me a message. Tell me what happened and which phone you use, and I'll get back to you.

Privacy

Chores is only used in my own house and isn't public. It only remembers which tasks are done each week, so everyone in the house sees the same thing.
