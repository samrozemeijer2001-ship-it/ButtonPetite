Lafbekken

A party game for one phone. Do you play it safe?

  • Live
  • iPhone
  • Web app
  • 18+

Open web app Code on GitHub

About

Pass one phone around the table. Every turn you pick LAF and play it safe, or LEF and go all in. The braver the table gets, the wilder it gets.

Why I made it

Game nights always ended with the same three games and the same discussion about the rules. I wanted something you can start in ten seconds, with one phone and zero setup, that gets more exciting the longer you play. LAF is Dutch for cowardly, LEF means guts. So every turn there's only one question: are you a chicken or are you brave?

What you can do

  1. 01

    Play it safe, or don't

    LAF is safe, until you pick it too often. Every LAF in a row makes the next one riskier. LEF is always fifty-fifty, but the stakes go up every time.

  2. 02

    Feel the heat rise

    Every brave choice turns up the heat. The colours and the mood of the game change with it, all the way to level 10.

  3. 03

    Double or nothing

    Win a LEF when things are already hot and you might get the chance to go double. Win, and two other players get a penalty. Lose, and it's on you.

  4. 04

    Rule cards, powers and forces

    Some cards stay on the table for a while and change the rules. Powers you play when it suits you. Forces block one choice for someone's next turn.

  5. 05

    124 cards, plus your own

    The game comes with 124 cards. You can change them, add your own and pick your own words for the small and the big penalty.

  6. 06

    Dutch or English

    Switch language on the start screen. Each language has its own set of cards.

  7. 07

    Pick up where you left off

    Close the app in the middle of a game and it's still there when you come back. It works without internet too.

Questions

How do I play?

Type in the names, then pass the phone around. On your turn, pick LAF to play it safe or LEF to go all in. The app tells you what happens next. That's it, go your gang.

How many players do I need?

Two or more. It gets more fun with a full table.

Why is it 18+?

Some cards are meant for adults. At the start of every game, the person holding the phone fills in their birth year. Please play responsibly and never push anyone to do something they don't want to do.

Can I make my own cards?

Yes. Go to Manage cards to change or add cards, and pick your own penalty words in the settings. Want the original cards back? There's a reset button at the bottom.

Are the English cards finished?

Almost. The Dutch cards came first, the English ones are newer. If a card sounds weird, send it to me and I'll fix it.

Something doesn't work. What now?

Send me a message with what you did, what went wrong and which phone you use. I fix bugs as fast as my evenings allow.

Need help?

Something broken or confusing? Send me a message. Tell me what happened and which phone you use, and I'll get back to you.

Privacy

Lafbekken doesn't collect any personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking. The web version only counts, anonymously, how often the game is played.
